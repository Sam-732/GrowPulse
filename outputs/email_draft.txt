Reusable support snippet (fee explainer)

- Brokerage is a fee Groww charges for processing a trade.
- Equity brokerage is ₹20 or 0.1% per executed order, whichever is lower, minimum ₹5.
- When selling shares, a DP charge of ₹20.00 (male) or ₹19.75 (female) is applied for debit values ₹100 or more, plus GST.
- Exchanges, SEBI, and the government charge fees such as stamp duty, exchange transaction charges, and SEBI turnover charges.
- GST at 18% is added on top of brokerage, DP charges, exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover charges, and other applicable fees.
- Account opening and maintenance charges are ₹0.
